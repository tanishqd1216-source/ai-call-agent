## What's already good (Module 19 — Multimodal Systems)

Worth stating explicitly, since this report is gap-heavy: the actual voice pipeline is the most
mature part of the whole system, not the weakest. Streaming STT/TTS, tuned VAD thresholds
(`SARVAM_PRE_SPEECH_PAD_FRAMES` tuned against a real observed clipping bug), per-turn latency
instrumentation with TTFT/TTS-TTFB breakdown, and prompt caching all match what
[`19-multimodal-systems.md`](../../Downloads/AI-Systems-Engineering-ClaudeCode/AI-Systems-Engineering/Generated/19-multimodal-systems.md)
and [`15-performance-engineering.md`](../../Downloads/AI-Systems-Engineering-ClaudeCode/AI-Systems-Engineering/Generated/15-performance-engineering.md)
describe as production-correct patterns. One thing worth a deliberate (not urgent) look: outbound
and inbound currently use different turn-detection tuning (`unlikely_threshold=0.40`/`max_delay=1.5s`
for outbound vs. framework defaults/`max_delay=2.5s` for inbound, confirmed via the shared-module
refactor's diff pass) — worth confirming this is a documented, deliberate choice (outbound is a
proactive callback so snappier interruption may be wanted; inbound may need more thinking time)
rather than undocumented drift, since it's exactly the kind of two-file divergence that's easy to
lose track of without the shared module in place yet.
